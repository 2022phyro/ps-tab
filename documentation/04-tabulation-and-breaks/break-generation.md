# Break & Finals Generation

At the conclusion of preliminary rounds, the tabulation engine applies configured break criteria to seed the elimination bracket.

---

## Break Cutoff Modes

* **Fixed Count**: Advance the top $N$ cumulative ranks (e.g., top $12$ advance to two Semifinal heats of $6$; top $6$ advance to Grand Finals).
* **Percentage**: Advance the top $X\%$ of the active field.
* **Equal Entry Advance**: The top $2$ speakers from every preliminary section advance automatically.

---

## Bubble Rounds

In tournaments running bubble rounds:
* Automatic qualifiers advance directly to the next stage.
* Borderline finishers (e.g., 3rd and 4th place) compete in a single **bubble round heat** to contest the remaining spots.
* Bubble rounds run through the standard scoring pipeline without requiring a separate outround algorithm.
