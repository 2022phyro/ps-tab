# Equity Desk & Compliance

Fairness in public speaking extends beyond mathematics. An open, respectful, and safe competitive environment is protected by Kwuo's dedicated **Equity Desk**.

---

## 1. Confidential Incident Workflow

The platform provides a secure mechanism for attendees to report conduct concerns:
- **Anonymous Reporting**: Speakers and adjudicators can submit reports without revealing their identity.
- **Direct Access**: Equity Officers access a private dashboard protected by specialized credentials.
- **Audit Logging**: Incident records track date, round flight, involved rooms, resolution notes, and sanctions while preserving party confidentiality.

---

## 2. Emergency Pairing Restraints

If an equity investigation requires parties to be kept apart:
- The Equity Officer can immediately enact an **Emergency Restraint**.
- The draw engine treats the restraint as a mandatory Hard Clash, preventing the individuals from being assigned to the same room or panel for the remainder of the tournament.
