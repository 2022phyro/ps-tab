# Staff & Officials

The **Staff** cockpit coordinates the organizing committee, tab room directors, equity officers, and logistical staff operating the tournament behind the scenes.

---

## 1. Staff Privacy & Public Display

In line with privacy regulations:
- Tab staff contact information (phone numbers and private email addresses) are strictly protected inside the secure administrative dashboard.
- The public overview page displays official names and roles without exposing personal phone numbers or direct email addresses.

---

## 2. Managing Staff Records

- **Adding Officials**: Click **+ Add Official** to invite a new committee member. Assign their operational title (e.g. *Deputy Chief Adjudicator*, *Tab Director*, *Equity Officer*) and permission role.
- **Setup & Invitation Links**: Click the link icon on any staff card to copy or resend their secure password initialization link.
- **Removing Staff**: Tab directors can revoke credentials and delete staff accounts directly from the table row actions menu or confirmation dialog.

---

## 3. Delegation Ties & Clashes

If a staff member also coaches or maintains ties to a specific delegation:
- Configure their **Primary & Secondary Delegation Ties** in their profile.
- This ensures any administrative decisions (such as manual panel adjustments) flag conflict warnings automatically.
