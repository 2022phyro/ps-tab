# Institutions & Delegations

Member institutions represent the foundational boundary for conflict-of-interest detection and regional tournament analytics.

---

## Delegation Properties

Every registered institution maintains:
- **Full Legal Name**: The official institution name (e.g., *Kwame Nkrumah University of Science and Technology*).
- **Institution Code**: A 2-to-6 character uppercase abbreviation used in high-density draw matrices (e.g., *KNUST*, *UI*, *AUC*).
- **Country & Flag**: Normalized country identification for international delegations.
- **Circuit / Regional Circuit**: Geographical grouping (e.g., *West Africa*, *Southern Africa*, *UK & Ireland*, *North America*) used for soft regional rotation.

---

## Conflict of Interest Clashes

When an institution record is assigned to a speaker or judge:
- **Hard Affiliation**: The system permanently marks any pairing between that judge and that speaker as an invalid draw.
- **Delegation Merges**: If attendees register under slight variations of the same school name (e.g., *Uni of Ibadan* vs *University of Ibadan*), select both delegations and click **Actions &rarr; Merge Delegations** to consolidate all affiliated speakers, adjudicators, and clash records into a single canonical entity.
