# Speakers & Divisions

The **Speakers** roster manages all registered competitors, mononyms/nicknames, multi-division eligibility, and electronic registration credentials.

---

## Competitor Attributes

- **Full Name & Display Name**: Full legal name alongside an optional stage name, mononym, or nickname.
- **Pronouns**: Formatted politely in round call sheets and judge scorecards (e.g., *she/her*, *they/them*).
- **Representing Institution**: The school, university, or academy with which the speaker is affiliated. Affiliations automatically generate **Hard Clashes** against judges from the same delegation.
- **Division Eligibility**: Speakers can be assigned to multiple competitive categories (e.g., *Open*, *Novice*, *ESL / EFL*, *Persuasive*, *Extemporaneous*).

---

## Adding Competitors

Tab staff have three methods for populating the speaker roster:

### 1. Direct Modal Entry
Click **+ Add Speaker** to create a single competitor profile with immediate division assignments.

### 2. Bulk Excel / CSV Upload
Click **Actions &rarr; Import Speakers** to upload a roster spreadsheet.
- Supported columns: `Name`, `Email`, `Phone`, `Institution`, `Divisions`, `Pronouns`.
- Missing institutions and divisions are created on the fly automatically.

### 3. Public Self-Registration Portal
Competitors can self-register directly via `/register` (when self-registration is enabled in tournament settings). Tab staff review submissions in the **Unconfirmed Registrations** drawer.

---

## Inline Editing & Fast Roster Corrections

The speakers table features high-efficiency inline editing:
- Click directly on any attendee name, institution, or contact cell to edit values without opening full-page forms.
- Press **Enter** to save changes immediately via HTMX background requests.
- Filter speakers instantly by typing into the sticky search bar or filtering by division.
