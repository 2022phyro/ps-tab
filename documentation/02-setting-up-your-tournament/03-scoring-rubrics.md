# Scoring Rubrics & Scales

Public speaking scoring differs fundamentally from debate team ballots. In Kwuo, an adjudicator scores each competitor against an explicit **multi-criteria matrix** that reflects expressive performance, content mastery, and rhetorical delivery.

---

## 1. Scale Boundaries & Anchors

The tournament configuration establishes universal scale boundaries:
- **Base Score**: The minimum floor score assigned for a minimally compliant speech (standard: `70.0`).
- **Ceiling Score**: The maximum attainable score representing a benchmark championship speech (standard: `100.0`).
- **Score Step / Granularity**: Scores are submitted to single-decimal precision (`0.1`), allowing fine differentiation between close performances.

---

## 2. Defining Rubric Criteria

Tab directors can customize the specific evaluation criteria evaluated on electronic ballots:

```
Total Speech Score (100 pts)
 ├── Content & Argumentation     (Weight: 40%)
 ├── Delivery & Rhetorical Voice  (Weight: 35%)
 └── Structure & Timing           (Weight: 25%)
```

### Criterion Attributes
- **Criterion Name**: Clear label displayed on the judge's mobile ballot card (e.g. *Delivery & Expression*).
- **Description & Indicators**: Descriptive rubric anchor text guiding adjudicators on what differentiates an 85 from a 95.
- **Maximum Points / Weight**: Maximum point allotment for this section.
- **Display Order**: The sequence criteria appear on digital scorecards.

---

## 3. Time Penalty Rules

Speaking rules prescribe minimum and maximum time limits (e.g., 5 to 7 minutes with a 30-second grace window):
- If configured, tab directors can set a **Maximum Time Penalty (e.g., 2.0 or 5.0 points)** applied deductively to speeches violating window parameters.
- Electronic ballots allow judges or room clerks to enter raw speech durations, automatically flagging time boundary infractions.
