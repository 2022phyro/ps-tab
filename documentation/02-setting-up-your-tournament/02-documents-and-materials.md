# Documents & Task Materials

The **Documents** tab allows tab staff to manage all digital collateral, competition briefs, judging manuals, and task prompts in one centralized repository.

---

## Document Categories

Every uploaded attachment is tagged with a distinct functional category:

| Category | Typical Documents | Visibility & Access |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Rules & Rubrics** | Official Tournament Rulebook, Rubric Matrices, Timekeeping policies | Publicly accessible in attendee document library |
| **Briefings** | Judge briefing slides, speaker guidelines, tab room procedures | Public / Attendee download |
| **Equity & Conduct** | Code of Conduct, incident reporting guidelines | Linked from public portal and equity desk |
| **Schedules** | Printable timetables, campus maps, room rosters | Public schedule page |
| **Task Materials** | Prepared speech prompts, impromptu motion dossiers, manuscript packets | Released automatically when round is published |
| **Awards & Breaks** | Tab sheets, break announcements, certificates | Released during Phase 4 conclusion |

---

## File Upload Requirements

- **Supported Formats**: PDF (`.pdf`), Microsoft Word (`.docx`), Excel spreadsheets (`.xlsx`, `.csv`), Text files (`.txt`), and images (`.png`, `.jpg`).
- **Storage Architecture**: Attachments are stored securely in the local filesystem storage engine and served via streaming binary responses with strict content-disposition headers.

> [!TIP]
> You can drag and drop multiple files directly into the upload dialog. Reorder documents by dragging their handle to configure the exact display hierarchy seen by competitors.
