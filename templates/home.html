Architectural Advantages

Why Kwuo Outperforms Legacy Debate Software

Built specifically for the mathematical and operational demands of individual speech tournaments, forensic showcases, and multi-speaker heats.

Dynamic Section Allocation

Partitions competitors into balanced heats between $[R_{\min}, R_{\max}]$ (e.g. 5 vs 6 speakers) without dummy entries or lopsided rooms. Strictly respects institutional clash separation.

Bounded Knapsack Room Balancing

Position-Bias Neutralization

Speaking first vs. last carries proven cognitive bias. Kwuo rotates speaker orders across preliminary rounds using Latin squares and cyclic shifts to equalize position variance.

Latin-Square Order Distribution

Flexible Ballot Pipelines

Supports Raw Sums, Weighted Panel Ranks, Trimmed Medians (dropping high/low outlier adjudications), and Z-score normalized ordinals for robust consensus.

Multi-Rubric Scoring Pipelines

Zero-Auth Mobile Ballots

Judges access scorecards by scanning a round QR code or opening a magic link. No accounts to register, zero passwords to recover in the tabroom hallway, and zero apps to install.

Instant Mobile Scorecard

State-Gated Visibility Controls

Draws, Scores, and Break Standings remain strictly decoupled. Tab directors can lock round draws, embargo specific rooms, and preview breaks before opening public visibility.

Audit Trails & Safe Release

Sub-10ms Async Performance

Built on Python 3.13, FastAPI ASGI, Redis caching, and binary MessagePack serialization. Scales gracefully to national championships with hundreds of live concurrent judges.
