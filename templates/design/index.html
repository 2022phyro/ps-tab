Component 02 Specification

The "Torn-Docket" Card

A physical paper container featuring deckled, organic torn edges on the top and bottom while preserving 100% straight vertical edges for grid alignment.

Grid Alignment Guarantee

Left and right vertical boundaries remain strictly at X = 0% and X = 100%. Multiple docket cards line up evenly in multi-column layouts without jagged side overlap.

Subtle Deckled Tear

Top and bottom cuts oscillate organically between 1px and 6px via clip-path: polygon(...). Content inside is insulated by py-8 px-6 padding.

Silhouette Drop Shadow

The clipped shape is housed within a .kwuo-torn-docket-wrapper utilizing filter: drop-shadow(...), casting shadows that hug the authentic torn contour.

Component 03 Specification

The "Wind-Curled" Post-It Note

Authentic physical memo sheets featuring liftoff corner curls, frosted cellophane tape strips, paper lighting gradients, and directional curl options (Left or Right).

Direction 1: Right-Corner Curl (curl="right")
Right Lift Curl Hover to uncurl

Round 3 Motion Drop at 14:15

Bottom-right corner sweeps upward by 22px with deep perspective shadow cast underneath. Cellophane tape holds top flat.

Class: kwuo-post-it kwuo-post-it-right
Direction 2: Left-Corner Curl (curl="left")
Left Lift Curl Hover to uncurl

Speaker Check-in Reminder

Mirrored alternating variant! Bottom-left corner lifts upward with left-angled shadow, perfect for alternating board arrangements.

Class: kwuo-post-it kwuo-post-it-left
Elliptical Corner Lift

Rather than a simple rounded rectangle, border-bottom-right-radius: 75px 22px creates an upward 22px curve along a 75px sweep, modeling physical paper curling.

Paper Lighting Gradient

A directional 135° / 225° gradient illuminates the curling corner with a natural highlight (light reflecting off the curved face) while shading the flatter anchor area.

Frosted Tape Teeth

.kwuo-tape-strip features translucent parchment tone with 1.5px dashed side borders simulating the serrated teeth of a physical tape dispenser.
