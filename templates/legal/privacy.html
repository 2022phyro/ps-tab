Legal & Data Governance

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026 • Effective for all Kwuo tournament instances

1. Fundamental Commitment to Participant Privacy

Kwuo operates under a strict data minimization philosophy. Competitive forensics should never become a vector for commercial tracking, advertising profiling, or invasive surveillance. We collect and process only the minimal data strictly required to run speech rounds, compute consensus scores, and produce certified tournament records.

2. Data Processed During Tournaments

When participating in a Kwuo-hosted tournament, the following data points may be processed:

  • Competitor Records: Competitor names, institutional affiliations (e.g. university/college), and division enrollment.
  • Adjudicator Records: Adjudicator names, institutional conflicts, and judge accreditation rankings.
  • Ballots & Scores: Numeric scores across rubric criteria, speaker ranks, qualitative feedback comments, and ballot submission timestamps.
  • Authentication Sessions: Hashed account passwords (Argon2id) and signed session cookies for tournament organizers and administrators.

3. Zero-Auth Magic Links & Ephemeral Access

Judges and speakers access ballots and personal schedules via cryptographic tokenized URLs (magic links). These tokens are ephemeral, tied directly to specific round heats, and do not track users across tournaments or store persistent third-party tracking cookies on mobile devices.

4. State-Gated Results Publishing

Ballot details, judge comments, and round results remain strictly confidential within the tournament adjudication core until the tab director explicitly executes a publishing gate. Results may be released publicly or held under embargo according to tournament rules.

5. Contact

For data protection inquiries, export requests, or erasure requests, contact our governance team at privacy@kwuo.org.
